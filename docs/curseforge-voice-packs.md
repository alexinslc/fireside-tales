## Fireside Tales Voices: Alliance

**Summary:** Hear Fireside Tales stories read aloud in the voices of Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Gnome, and Skyborne tellers.

**Description:**

When a Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Gnome, or Skyborne player near you tells a Fireside Tales story, each line plays aloud in the voice of their race, a man's or a woman's. You hear your own stories too.

This pack needs Fireside Tales. Only listeners need it: the teller doesn't.

**What's in it**
- 10 voices, a man and a woman for each of Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Gnome, and Skyborne.
- Every line of every built-in story, about 2,580 lines for each voice.
- About 860 MB.

**Other tellers**
- Orc, Undead, Tauren, and Troll tellers are in Fireside Tales Voices: Horde.
- Fireside Tales Voices: Narrator reads any teller whose race pack you don't have.

**Settings**
Open the story menu (/tales) and its Voices tab, the gear at the bottom of the tabs. Choose race voices or one narrator for every story, turn your own stories on or off, and play a sample. Voices play at the game's Master volume.

**Good to know**
The voices are AI-generated: designed with ElevenLabs and recorded with fish.audio. They aren't the game's voice actors. Tales you write for your own character aren't read aloud. This pack is free, and it isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.

## Fireside Tales Voices: Horde

**Summary:** Hear Fireside Tales stories read aloud in the voices of Orc, Undead, Tauren, Troll, and Skyborne tellers.

**Description:** the same as Alliance, with:
- Orc, Undead, Tauren, Troll, and Skyborne in place of the Alliance races.
- About 910 MB.
- "Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, and Gnome tellers are in Fireside Tales Voices: Alliance."

## Fireside Tales Voices: Narrator

**Summary:** One narrator reads every Fireside Tales story aloud, whoever tells it.

**Description:**

When someone near you tells a Fireside Tales story, each line is read aloud by the narrator. With a race pack installed too, the narrator reads only the tellers whose race voice you don't have. Choose Narrator in the Voices tab to hear him read every story.

This pack needs Fireside Tales. Only listeners need it: the teller doesn't.

**What's in it**
- The narrator, a man's voice.
- Every line of every built-in story, about 2,580 lines.
- About 85 MB.

**Race voices**
- Fireside Tales Voices: Alliance has Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Gnome, and Skyborne.
- Fireside Tales Voices: Horde has Orc, Undead, Tauren, Troll, and Skyborne.

**Settings** and **Good to know**: the same as Alliance.

## Fireside Tales Voices: Community

**Summary:** Hear each week's new Fireside Tales community stories read aloud, in the voice of whoever tells them.

**Description:**

Players share new stories at firesidetales.dev, and each week up to 100 are featured in Fireside Tales' Community tab. This pack reads them aloud. When someone near you tells one, each line plays in the voice of the teller's race, or the narrator's if you've chosen him.

This pack needs Fireside Tales. Only listeners need it: the teller doesn't. It updates each week with the new stories, so keep it up to date with the CurseForge app.

**What's in it**
- The lines of this week's community stories, and the last few weeks', in every voice: the narrator, and a man and a woman for each of Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Gnome, Skyborne, Orc, Undead, Tauren, and Troll.
- About 20 MB, more in a busy week.

The built-in stories are in the Alliance, Horde, and Narrator packs.

**Settings** and **Good to know**: the same as Alliance.
