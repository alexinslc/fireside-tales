# Artwork brief

CurseForge requires the logo to be original. It can't use a game's logo or a generic game graphic, or copy another project's logo.

## What's needed

| Asset | Size | Needed for | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Project logo | 1024x1024 PNG, square | The CurseForge page and search results | Required |
| In-game icon | The same art, simplified, 512x512 PNG with a transparent background | The round window portrait, the AddOns list | Recommended |
| Minimap icon | 512x512 PNG with a transparent background, readable at about 20 pixels | The minimap button | Nice to have |
| Banner | 1280x400 PNG, with room for the title | The top of the description | Nice to have |

Send PNGs at these sizes. The in-game versions get converted to the format and sizes the game needs.

## Art direction

- **The idea:** an open, well-worn storybook beside a small campfire at night, with sparks rising from the fire and curling into the air above the pages, as if the story is lifting off them.
- **The mood:** warm, inviting, and a little mysterious. The feeling of leaning in to hear a good tale.
- **The look:** hand-painted fantasy art with chunky shapes and bold, readable silhouettes. Amber firelight against deep blue-black shadow, with touches of aged bronze.
- **Readable when small:** one clear subject, high contrast, and no fine detail that gets lost at icon size. The logo should still make sense at 64x64 pixels.

## Things to avoid

- World of Warcraft logos, faction crests, or text from the game.
- Blizzard characters or anything copied from official art.
- Other addons' logos.
- Trying to pass AI art off as a screenshot. Gallery screenshots must be real, from the game.

## Prompts to paste

**Project logo:**

> A square game icon of an open, well-worn leather storybook lying beside a small campfire at night. Glowing sparks rise from the fire and curl above the open pages, as if the story is lifting off them. Hand-painted fantasy art with chunky shapes and a bold silhouette, amber firelight against deep blue-black shadow, aged bronze accents. One clear central subject, high contrast, readable at small sizes. No text, no logos, no people.

**In-game icon:**

> The same open storybook and small flame, simplified into a single bold icon, centered, with a clean silhouette and strong contrast, on a transparent background. Keep everything well inside a centered circle, because the corners will be cropped. No text, no logos.

**Minimap icon:**

> A very simple icon of an open book with one small flame above it, on a transparent background. Extremely bold shapes and strong contrast, readable at 20 pixels. Warm amber and bronze. No text, no logos.

**Banner (optional):**

> A wide scene at night: a small campfire in a forest clearing with an open storybook on a log beside it, sparks drifting up into the dark, travelers' silhouettes seated around the fire. Hand-painted fantasy art, amber firelight against deep blue-black shadow, with open dark space on the right side for a title. No text, no logos, no recognizable characters.
