# Changelog

## 1.3.0

- **Tell a Tale** picks a story for you: one you haven't told yet, about where you are if it can. Press it again for another.
- **By the fire:** settle by a campfire and the teller bar offers a story, with **Speak**, **Another**, and a close button. It asks once per fire, at most every 10 minutes, never in combat or mid-story, and goes away by itself after 30 seconds. `/tales fire` turns it off.
- The reader shows roughly how long each story takes to tell.
- The story count moved to the title bar, next to the version.
- Restart the game once after updating, since a file was added.

## 1.2.0

- 46 new action and wise stories, for 534 in all. Every zone now has at least four, and every race and class has a new action story built on its own abilities.
- The story menu shows the addon's version in its title bar, and `/tales status` includes it.

## 1.1.1

- Removed the pop-up that offered a line after quests, level-ups, new zones, and deaths, along with its Skip key binding and the `/tales on`, `off`, `cooldown`, and `test` commands. Stories, the teller bar, and its reading cue are unchanged.
- Restart the game once after updating, since a file was removed.

## 1.1.0

- 81 new stories, for 488 in all. Moonglade, Deadwind Pass, Mount Hyjal, the Riverglades, and Shen'dralas have stories for the first time, Dalaran has three under Alterac Mountains, and 36 other zones each get a new one.
- New stories for races, classes, every theme, and telling to someone by name.
- Removed 16 stories that repeated others.
- Emotes now all work while sitting at any fire, with no feet, boots, snow, or moon required.
- Fixed lore for Forever: Dalaran's dome is down, Skyborne glide rather than fly, and a few places and creatures are where they belong.

## 1.0.1

- Added the game's own sounds for opening and closing windows, picking a story, and using sections, filters, favorites, and the list for tonight.

## 1.0.0

The first release.

- More than 400 stories to tell at the campfire, for every race, class, and zone, plus stories told to a listener by name.
- **Write My Story:** describe your character, copy a ready-made prompt into any AI, and paste back your own stories.
- Press **Speak** for each line. A small teller bar shows the next line, a cue to let people read, and what's being said nearby.
- Find stories by search, source, or mood, with favorites and a list for tonight.
