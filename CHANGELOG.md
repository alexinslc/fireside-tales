# Changelog

## 1.7.0

- **Ten new legends** in the Legends tab, retold from Warcraft's own history:
  - **The Ranger-General** (Sylvanas at Quel'Thalas, 2 parts)
  - **Two Boy Kings** (Anduin and Varian, 2 parts)
  - **The Night They Gave Up Forever** (the Battle of Mount Hyjal, 2 parts)
  - **Tirion and the Orc** (Tirion and Eitrigg, 2 parts)
  - **The Last Breath of Grom Hellscream** (Demon Fall, 3 parts)
  - **The Lightbringer** (Uther)
  - **The Name the Boy King Carries** (Anduin Lothar)
  - **The Long Wait** (Tyrande Whisperwind, 2 parts)
  - **The Island the Wind Held** and **The Day the Wind Went Quiet** (two Skyborne histories)
- A legend told in one sitting no longer says "part 1 of 1".
- **Community tab:** its first week starts Tuesday, November 17, with ten new tales and rumors. Share your own at firesidetales.dev.
- 637 stories in all, counting the legends' parts.
- **Special thanks to tmillerpdx for writing and contributing all ten new legends!**

## 1.6.0

- **Community tab:** a new tab of stories from other players, new every week and credited to their authors. This week's are at the top, with recent weeks below, and **Tell a Tale** can pick from this week's too.
- **Contribute a Story:** on the Community tab, this button shows where to share a story or rumor of your own: **firesidetales.dev**. Write it in your browser, see how it'll look in chat, and each week up to 100 are featured for every player. If yours is featured, you'll see a note in chat when you log in.
- **Write Your Tales:** Write My Story is now called Write Your Tales, to match the **Your Tales** tab where its stories go. Those are still just for you.
- The tab icons fill their tabs, like the character window's.
- Restart the game once after updating, since a file was added.

## 1.5.0

- **Tabs:** the story menu has tabs down its right side, like the character window: **Your Tales** with your character's portrait, **Stories**, **Legends**, and **Rumors and Whispers**, each with the game's own icons.
- **Tell, then Speak:** in the menu, **Tell** moves a story to the teller bar without saying anything, so you can start with an opener. Each row now shows roughly how many seconds a story takes instead of its line count, hovering a row explains it, and the note icon on your own stories is gone, since their titles are tinted and they have their own tab.
- **Openers and closers:** before a story, **Opener** gathers people with one line. After it, **Closer** sends it off, matched to the story's mood. Hover to see the line first. Each is said only when you press it, once per story.
- **Legends:** Warcraft's own stories, told in parts. When a part ends, Speak becomes **Next part**, so you can stop for the night or keep going. Five to start, each from its classic quests:
  - **The Legend of Stalvan** (Duskwood, 4 parts)
  - **Tirion Fordring: In Dreams** (Western Plaguelands, 5 parts)
  - **The Tower of Althalaxx** (Darkshore, 4 parts)
  - **The Legend of Dartol's Rod** (Ashenvale, 4 parts)
  - **Onyxia: The Great Masquerade** (Burning Steppes, 4 parts)
- **Night tales:** a spooky or dark story each for Elwynn Forest, Durotar, Darnassus, Feralas, Un'Goro Crater, and Zephras Isle, which had none, so **Tell a Tale** has something for after dark in every zone.
- **Rumors and Whispers:** 24 short rumors for everyone, about half a minute each. Strange bells, a chair nobody sits in, a vault combination that ends in a sneeze, a kodo on the council, and a dozen about love: the Deeprun Tram kiss, a matchmaking goblin, and cold roses in Darkshire.
- **Winter Veil:** 7 more tales, including Greatfather Winter in two cities at once, the Smokywood Pastures ledger, a runaway reindeer, and the first spooky and dark ones for the season's long nights.
- **Story fixes:** 27 stories had a line that didn't make sense out loud, like a callback to something never mentioned or a detail that contradicted itself earlier in the story. They read cleanly now.
- The "Tell a tale?" pop-up at a campfire is now called the campfire prompt everywhere, including the message when you turn it off with `/tales fire`.
- 619 stories in all, counting the legends' parts. Restart the game once after updating, since files were added.
- **Coming soon:** mini-games by the fire, starting with riddles that people around you answer in `/say`.

## 1.4.0

- **Festival stories:** 27 new tales for the Darkmoon Faire, Lunar Festival, Love is in the Air, Noblegarden, Children's Week, Midsummer, the Harvest Festival, Hallow's End, and Winter Veil, for 561 in all. You'll find them under **From: Festivals** all year.
- While a festival is on, its stories open at the top of the list, **Tell a Tale** picks them first, and the campfire prompt mentions it, such as "The Darkmoon Faire is in town. Tell a tale?" The addon reads the game's calendar, or knows the Darkmoon Faire's week and the dates of the fixed festivals.
- At night in the game, **Tell a Tale** and the fire reach for spooky and dark tales first, and gentler ones by day.
- **Total RP 3:** if you use it, Write My Story puts your current profile into the prompt: name, title, appearance, where you're from, what you're doing, and your backstory. Edit your profile and the prompt follows. The box for your own words is still there for anything extra.
- **Fixed:** the campfire prompt (the "Tell a tale?" pop-up when you sit at a fire) now shows up at WoW Forever's campsites, which give Welcoming Campfire rather than Cozy Fire, and it notices a fire you're already sitting by when you log in. Walk away and come back, and it can offer again after 5 minutes instead of 10.
- Restart the game once after updating, since files were added.

## 1.3.0

- **Tell a Tale** picks a story for you: one you haven't told yet, about where you are if it can. Press it again for another.
- **Campfire prompt:** sit down at a campfire and a small prompt on the teller bar asks if you'd like to tell a story, with **Speak**, **Another**, and a close button. It asks once per fire, at most every 10 minutes, never in combat or mid-story, and goes away by itself after 30 seconds. `/tales fire` turns it off.
- The reader shows roughly how long each story takes to tell.
- The story count moved to the title bar, next to the version.
- Restart the game once after updating, since a file was added.

## 1.0.0

The first release.

- More than 400 stories to tell at the campfire, for every race, class, and zone, plus stories told to a listener by name.
- **Write My Story:** describe your character, copy a ready-made prompt into any AI, and paste back your own stories.
- Press **Speak** for each line. A small teller bar shows the next line, a cue to let people read, and what's being said nearby.
- Find stories by search, source, or mood, with favorites and a list for tonight.
