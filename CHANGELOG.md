# Changelog

## 1.9.1

- Fixed the Delete and Restart buttons overlapping.

## 1.9.0

- Added Edit for your own tales, so you can change one without pasting it again.

## 1.8.0

- Added a Riddles tab with 22 riddles. Ask one at the fire, and whoever says the answer first wins.
- If nobody gets it, press Speak for a hint, then again for the answer.
- Restart the game after updating.

## 1.7.1

- Removed the placeholder stories from the Community tab.

## 1.7.0

- Added 10 legends: Sylvanas, Anduin and Varian, Mount Hyjal, Tirion and Eitrigg, Grom Hellscream, Uther, Anduin Lothar, Tyrande, and two Skyborne histories.
- Single-part legends no longer show "part 1 of 1".
- **Special thanks to tmillerpdx for writing all ten!**

## 1.6.0

- Added a Community tab with new stories from players every week.
- Added Contribute a Story, which points you to firesidetales.dev.
- You'll get a message in chat when one of your stories is featured.
- Renamed Write My Story to Write Your Tales.
- Restart the game after updating.

## 1.5.0

- Added side tabs for Your Tales, Stories, Legends, and Rumors and Whispers.
- Tell now puts a story on the teller bar first, so you can start with an Opener and finish with a Closer.
- Added 5 legends: Stalvan, In Dreams, the Tower of Althalaxx, Dartol's Rod, and Onyxia.
- Added 24 rumors, 7 Winter Veil stories, and a night story for every zone.
- Fixed 27 stories with lines that didn't make sense.

## 1.4.0

- Added 27 festival stories. They show up first while their festival is on.
- Tell a Tale picks spooky and dark stories at night.
- Write My Story can use your Total RP 3 profile.
- Fixed the campfire prompt not showing at WoW: Forever campsites.

## 1.3.0

- Added Tell a Tale, which picks a story you haven't told yet.
- Added a campfire prompt when you sit by a fire. Turn it off with `/tales fire`.
- The reader shows about how long a story takes.

## 1.2.0

- Added 46 action and wise stories. Every zone has at least four now.
- The version number shows in the menu and in `/tales status`.

## 1.1.1

- Removed the pop-up that suggested lines after quests, level-ups, and deaths.

## 1.1.0

- Added 81 stories, including the first ones for Moonglade, Deadwind Pass, Mount Hyjal, the Riverglades, and Shen'dralas.
- Removed 16 duplicate stories.
- Every emote works while sitting now.
- Fixed some lore details for WoW: Forever.

## 1.0.1

- Added sounds for windows, picking a story, and filters.

## 1.0.0

- First release, with over 400 stories for every race, class, and zone.
- Write My Story helps you write your own character's stories with any AI.
- Search, filters, favorites, and a list for tonight.
